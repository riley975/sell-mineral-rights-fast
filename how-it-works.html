How selling mineral rights works

You deal directly with the buyer, from the first call through payment.

We have your request.

Someone from Dynasty Mineral Partners will contact you about a cash offer. You do not have to accept it. To talk now, call (817) 818-1034.

No cost. No obligation.

Request a cash offer

About a minute. No fee to request an offer.

No fee. No obligation to sell.

1. Tell us what you own

Call (817) 818-1034 or use the form. We will ask where the minerals are, whether you receive royalty checks, and how you came to own the interest. Inherited, small, and fractional shares are included.

A check stub, division order, lease, or deed is useful. You do not need any of them to start.

2. We review the interest

There is no single price per acre. We look at the wells, production, the lease, your decimal, and nearby permits or drilling. Producing interests, near-term interests, and undeveloped locations are priced differently because the risk is different.

Much of our work is in the Permian Basin, the Red Fork and Cherokee of the Anadarko Basin, the DJ Basin in Weld County, the Powder River Basin in Converse County, and the Bakken. We also purchase interests in other producing areas of the United States.

3. You receive a written offer

We send a written offer, usually within a few business days of having enough information. You can review it with your family, an attorney, or a tax advisor. You do not have to accept it.

You can sell all of an interest or only part of it.

4. Title and closing

If you accept, we order title and prepare the closing documents. That is usually a mineral deed or a royalty deed, depending on what you own. Most closings finish within a few weeks. County title work sets the timeline.

We are not your attorney. Have your own lawyer review the deed before you sign if you want that review.

5. You are paid

When the deed is signed and closing is complete, you are paid. The operator is notified so later royalties follow the new owner. If you kept a portion, that portion stays in your name.

What it costs

Nothing, to find out. There is no fee for an offer and no broker commission. A sale can have tax consequences that depend on your basis and how you acquired the interest. That is a question for your tax advisor.

Dynasty Mineral Partners is a mineral and royalty interest acquisition company. We are not attorneys or tax advisors, and nothing on this website is legal, tax, or financial advice.

Common questions

Do I need paperwork before I contact you?

No. The state, the county, and what you know about the interest are enough to start. Check stubs, division orders, leases, and deeds make the review faster when you have them.

Can I sell only part of my interest?

Yes. Some owners sell a portion and keep the rest. The offer describes what would transfer.

Who pays for title work?

If you accept an offer, we order the title work and prepare the closing documents as part of the purchase. There is no separate fee for the offer.
