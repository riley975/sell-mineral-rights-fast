How selling your mineral rights works

Five steps from a phone call to getting paid. You are talking to the buyer, not a broker listing your interest for someone else.

We have your request.

Someone from Dynasty Mineral Partners will contact you about a cash offer. You do not have to accept it. To talk now, call (817) 818-1034.

Step 1. Tell us what you own

Call (817) 818-1034 or use the form. We will ask where the minerals are, whether you receive royalty checks, and how you came to own the interest. Inherited, small, and fractional shares are welcome. A check stub, division order, lease, or deed helps us move faster. You do not need any of them to start.

Step 2. We evaluate the specific interest

There is no honest flat price per acre. We look at public records and production data for your tract: which wells it is tied to, how those wells have produced, the lease terms, your decimal, and nearby permits or drilling. Producing interests (PDP), near-term interests (a permit or a drilled-but-uncompleted well), and undeveloped locations (PUD) are priced differently because the risk is different.

Our buy box is the Permian Basin in Texas and New Mexico, Red Fork and Cherokee in the Oklahoma Anadarko, the DJ Basin in Weld County, Colorado, the Powder River Basin in Converse County, Wyoming, and occasionally the Bakken in Montana and North Dakota. If your county is outside that, we will say so.

Step 3. You get a written cash offer

We send a written offer, usually within a few business days of having enough to evaluate the interest. Read it. Talk it over with family, an attorney, or a tax advisor. Nothing in the offer requires you to accept it.

You can sell all of an interest or only part of it. Some owners take cash for a portion and keep the rest of the minerals in the family.

Step 4. Title and closing

If you accept, we order title, work through curative items, and prepare the closing documents — typically a mineral deed or a royalty deed, depending on what you own. Most closings finish within a few weeks. County title is what usually sets the timeline, not a countdown on our side.

We are not your attorney. Have your own lawyer review the deed before you sign if you want that review. We expect you to.

Step 5. You are paid

When the deed is signed and closing is complete, you are paid and the operator is notified of the change in ownership so later royalties follow the new owner. If you kept a portion, that portion stays in your name.

What it costs you

Nothing, to find out. There is no fee for an offer and no broker commission, because we are the buyer. Selling can have tax consequences that depend on your basis, how you inherited the interest, and the rest of your situation. That is a question for your tax advisor, not for us.

Dynasty Mineral Partners is a mineral and royalty interest acquisition company. We are not attorneys or tax advisors, and nothing on this website is legal, tax, or financial advice.

Common questions

Do I need paperwork before I contact you?

No. A county and whatever you know about the interest is enough to start. Check stubs, division orders, leases, and deeds make the evaluation faster when you have them.

Can I sell only part of my interest?

Yes. Some owners sell a portion for cash now and keep the rest. The offer will describe exactly what would transfer.

Who pays for title work?

If you accept an offer, we coordinate and cover the title work and closing documents as part of buying the interest. There is no separate fee to you for the offer itself.
