Who we are

This website is operated by Dynasty Land & Minerals, LLC, doing business as Dynasty Mineral Partners (“we,” “us”), based in Weatherford, Texas. Contact: riley@dynastylm.com, (817) 818-1034.

Information we collect

When you submit the offer form, we collect what you provide: name, phone number, email if you include one, state, county, whether you receive royalty checks, and the page you submitted from. We also collect standard technical data, such as browser type and pages visited, through cookies used by our analytics and advertising tags.

How we use it

We use your information to evaluate your mineral interest, contact you about an offer, and operate and measure this website and our advertising.

Calls and texts

If you consent on the form, we may contact you by phone, text, or email, including by automated means. Consent is not a condition of any offer. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out of texts by replying STOP, and you can tell us you no longer want to be contacted.

How the form is delivered

Submissions are sent to us by email or through a form service we configure. That provider receives the information you submit so it can deliver the message to us. Until that delivery is configured, the form asks you to call or email instead of showing a confirmation.

Analytics and advertising

We use Google Analytics (measurement ID G-SCQXCMMNTG) and the Meta (Facebook) Pixel to understand traffic and measure ads. Those services may use cookies. You can manage ad settings through Google and Meta and through your browser.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it with service providers who help us operate, with parties involved in a closing you agree to, or when the law requires it.

Your choices

To access, correct, or delete your information, email riley@dynastylm.com.

Changes
