One basin, two states

The Permian Basin produces from West Texas and southeastern New Mexico. We buy mineral interests, royalty interests, and overriding royalties across that basin, producing and not yet producing.

State notes are on the Texas and New Mexico pages. If you own in both states, one conversation can cover both.

Midland Basin

The Midland Basin is in Texas. Counties we review often include Midland, Martin, Howard, Glasscock, Reagan, and Upton. We look at the wells that are paying, the ones that are permitted or drilled but not completed, and locations that are still undeveloped.

Delaware Basin

The Delaware Basin is the western Permian. In Texas that includes counties such as Reeves, Loving, Ward, Winkler, Pecos, and Culberson. In New Mexico it is principally Lea County and Eddy County.

What we need from you

The county, and as much of the legal description as you have. A royalty check stub is the fastest picture of a producing decimal. If you have never received a check, say that. Inherited and fractional shares are common.

How the offer is made

We research the interest and send a written offer, usually within a few business days. You can decline it. If you accept, we coordinate title and the deed. Texas deeds are recorded with the county clerk. Railroad Commission records cover the wells. In New Mexico, well records sit with the Oil Conservation Division and deeds with the county clerk. You do not need to order any of that before you call (817) 818-1034.

No fee, no broker, and no obligation. Choose the state where the minerals are. If you own in both Texas and New Mexico, pick one and name the other county in the county field.

Questions about the Permian

What is the difference between the Midland and Delaware basins?

They are the two halves of the Permian. The Midland Basin is the eastern side, in Texas. The Delaware Basin is the western side, in Texas and in Lea and Eddy counties, New Mexico. We buy in both.

Do you buy only producing Permian wells?

No. We buy producing interests, near-term interests such as permits and drilled but uncompleted wells, and undeveloped locations, along with overriding royalties.

Do you buy outside the Permian?

Yes. The Permian is a large part of our work. We also buy mineral and royalty interests in other U.S. oil and gas areas.
