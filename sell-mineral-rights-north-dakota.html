Western North Dakota, occasionally

The Bakken in western North Dakota — counties such as McKenzie and Williams, where Bakken development has been concentrated — is an occasional part of the Dynasty buy box. We look at mineral interests, royalty interests, and overriding royalties: PDP, permits and DUCs, and undeveloped locations. We do not treat the whole state as in-bounds, and we do not treat every Bakken decimal as one we will buy.

If you want a straight answer, call (817) 818-1034 or send the form with the county. You will get a yes or a no, not a vague “we buy nationwide.”

Montana is the same trend

Eastern Montana Bakken interests follow the same rule and are described on the Montana page. Ownership that straddles the line can be submitted once. Name both counties if you know them.

Records

The North Dakota Department of Mineral Resources, Oil and Gas Division, holds well and production information. Deeds are recorded with the county recorder where the minerals are located. A stub or division order skips a lot of searching, and you can start without one.

Inherited shares

Bakken minerals are often split among heirs who live far from the wells. We buy those shares when the interest itself fits, and one heir can sell without the others. There is no fee to ask and no obligation to accept a written offer. Tax treatment of an inherited interest is a question for your tax advisor. More on inherited minerals.

No fees. No broker. No obligation. If it is not a fit, we will say so.

Questions about North Dakota

Which North Dakota counties?

The Bakken in western North Dakota, including counties such as McKenzie and Williams where Bakken wells are concentrated. The rest of the state is outside the current buy box.

Is every Bakken interest a fit?

No. These purchases are occasional. We look at producing interests, near-term wells, and undeveloped locations, then tell you whether we can make an offer.

Do you buy small mineral interests in North Dakota?

Yes, when we are buying in that area. Fractional and inherited shares are welcome to submit.
