A note owners ask about anyway

Louisiana oil and gas rights are not the same as Texas minerals. Under the Louisiana Mineral Code, a mineral right created apart from the land is generally a mineral servitude, and a servitude can be extinguished by prescription after ten years of nonuse. Whether a particular servitude is still alive depends on the facts of production and use. That is a legal question for a Louisiana attorney, not something this website can answer for your tract.

Parishes, not counties, are the local unit. Land records are kept by the clerk of court in the parish, and well data is published through the state’s SONRIS system. We are not your lawyer, and nothing on this page is legal advice.
