Our Texas buy box is the Permian

Texas mineral ownership is often old, split, and inherited. We buy from individual and family owners, not only from companies with a full leasehold. The interests we want in Texas are in the Permian Basin: producing wells (PDP), near-term development (permits and drilled-but-uncompleted wells), and proved undeveloped locations (PUD). Minerals, royalties, and overriding royalties all fit.

Midland Basin and the Texas Delaware

The Midland Basin is the eastern Permian. Counties we look at regularly include Midland, Martin, Howard, Glasscock, Reagan, and Upton. The Delaware Basin is the western Permian. On the Texas side that includes counties such as Reeves, Loving, Ward, Winkler, Pecos, and Culberson. The New Mexico side of the Delaware is Lea and Eddy counties, covered on our New Mexico page. A longer look at both sides is on the Permian Basin page.

If your tract is Permian but the county is not named here, call anyway. If it is Eagle Ford, Barnett, Haynesville, or elsewhere in East or South Texas, it is outside the current buy box. We will say that plainly.

Records that describe a Texas interest

Oil and gas production and permitting in Texas run through the Railroad Commission of Texas. Deeds and leases are recorded with the county clerk in the county where the minerals are. A recent royalty stub plus the legal description — abstract, survey, or section — is usually enough for us to find the rest.

Questions about Texas

Do you buy Eagle Ford, Barnett, or East Texas minerals?

Those plays are outside our current Texas buy box. Our Texas focus is the Permian. Call and we will tell you directly if an interest elsewhere in Texas is something we can look at.

Which Texas counties do you focus on?

Midland Basin counties such as Midland, Martin, Howard, Glasscock, Reagan, and Upton, and Delaware Basin counties such as Reeves, Loving, Ward, Winkler, Pecos, and Culberson. Other Permian counties are worth a call.
