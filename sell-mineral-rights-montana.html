Paperwork and title

A royalty stub, division order, or deed is enough to start. Deeds are recorded with the clerk and recorder in the county where the minerals are. Oil and gas wells are regulated by the Montana Board of Oil and Gas Conservation. You do not need to pull those records yourself.
