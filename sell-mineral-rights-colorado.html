Weld County and the DJ Basin

The Denver-Julesburg Basin is where we are most active in Colorado, and Weld County is the center of that work. We buy mineral interests, royalty interests, and overriding royalties: wells that are producing, permits, drilled but uncompleted wells, and undeveloped locations.

Other Colorado interests

If your minerals are in another Colorado county, send them. If the numbers make sense, we will make an offer. That includes other DJ Basin counties and other Colorado basins.

These notes describe where we are most active. If the numbers make sense, we will buy mineral and royalty interests in any U.S. oil and gas area.

What a DJ interest often looks like

Many Weld County owners inherited a fractional interest. The mail they receive is a royalty statement or a lease offer. A stub with the well name and your decimal is enough to start. You can sell your share without the other owners selling theirs, if title is in your name.

Records

Colorado oil and gas operations are regulated by the Energy and Carbon Management Commission. Deeds and leases are recorded with the county clerk and recorder. We use both the well records and the county record when we write an offer.

Questions about Colorado

Do you buy small inherited interests in the DJ Basin?

Yes. Fractional and inherited shares are included.
