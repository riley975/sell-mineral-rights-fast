Common Questions

Are there any fees to get an offer?

No. There is never a cost to submit your information or receive a cash offer, and you are never obligated to accept.

Do you buy small or fractional mineral interests?

Yes. We buy interests of any size, including small fractional and inherited shares that can be difficult to sell through a traditional broker.

Do I have to sell all of my mineral rights?

No. Many owners sell only a portion of their mineral or royalty interest and keep the rest. We can discuss what makes sense for your situation.

How long does the process take?

Once you accept an offer, most closings are completed within a few weeks, depending on title work in your county. We walk you through each step.
