What Determines The Value Of Your Mineral Rights?

Every mineral interest is different. Your offer can depend on several factors, including:

Current royalty income
Your ownership percentage
Historical production
The county and geological formation
Producing wells and nearby drilling activity
Lease and ownership terms
Future development potential
Recent market activity and comparable transactions

We evaluate the specific characteristics of your mineral interest rather than giving you a generic per-acre estimate.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you determine what my mineral rights are worth?

We look at factors like current royalty income, historical production, nearby drilling activity, lease terms, ownership percentage, and market comparables in your specific county to arrive at a fair cash offer.

Do you buy small or fractional mineral interests?

Yes. We buy interests of any size, including small fractional and inherited shares that can be difficult to sell through a traditional broker.

Are there any fees to get an offer?

No. There is never a cost to submit your information or receive a cash offer. You're never obligated to accept.

Do I have to sell all of my mineral rights?

No. Many owners choose to sell only a portion of their mineral or royalty interest and keep the rest. We can discuss what makes sense for your situation.

Do I need to hire a broker?

No. You're dealing directly with us as the buyer, so there's no broker to hire and no commission to pay. We handle the evaluation, offer, and closing process ourselves.

How long does the process take?

Once you accept an offer, most closings are completed within a few weeks, depending on title work in your county. We'll walk you through each step.

What if I don't know exactly what I own?

That's common, especially with inherited interests. Tell us what you do know — like the county or a division order — and we'll help you figure out the rest.

What if I'm currently receiving royalty checks?

That's fine — we buy producing interests as well as non-producing and inherited ones. Receiving royalty checks doesn't affect your ability to get an offer.

What happens after I accept an offer?

We coordinate the title work, curative work, and closing paperwork, and keep you updated at each step until you get paid.

Do you buy inherited mineral rights?

Yes. Inherited interests, including those split among multiple heirs, are some of the most common interests we buy.

What if I decide not to sell?

That's completely fine. Getting an offer doesn't obligate you to accept it, and there's never any pressure to move forward.
