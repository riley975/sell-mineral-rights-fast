How an offer works

The purchase is made with us, not through a listing. The full sequence is here.

  1. 1

    Tell us about the interest

    Call or use the form. We need the state, the county, and whether royalty checks are being paid. A check stub, division order, or deed lets us identify the wells sooner. None of those are required to start.

  2. 2

    We prepare a written offer

    We review production, the lease, and nearby drilling, then send a written offer, usually within a few business days. You may review it with your family, your attorney, or your tax advisor. Acceptance is your decision.

  3. 3

    Closing

    If you accept, we order title and prepare the deed. Most closings are completed within a few weeks. The county’s title work sets the schedule. Payment is made when closing is finished.

What we buy

Mineral interests, royalty interests, and overriding royalty interests.

Producing interests

These are proved developed producing properties. The wells are on production, and the owner is often receiving a royalty check. We price them from the income, the decline, and the lease terms.

Permits and drilled wells

A permit may be on file, or the well may be drilled and waiting on completion. We underwrite those interests from the expected well, not from a check that has not started.

Undeveloped locations

Proved undeveloped locations are not producing yet. Value depends on where the acreage sits and what an operator can still drill there.

Royalties and overrides

We buy mineral interests, leasehold royalties, and overriding royalty interests. An override is carved from a lease and generally ends when that lease ends.

Inherited and fractional shares

Many of the interests we buy were inherited and are owned in fractions. One heir may sell a share without the other owners selling theirs.

Working with us

Common questions

All frequently asked questions

Is there a fee to request an offer?

No. There is no fee to tell us about an interest or to receive a written offer. You do not have to accept it. We are the buyer, so there is no broker fee.

Do you buy small, fractional, or inherited interests?

Yes. Inherited minerals, family shares, and fractional interests are part of what we buy. You do not need a large acreage position to request an offer.

Do I have to sell all of my mineral rights?

No. Many owners sell part of a mineral or royalty interest and keep the rest. The offer will say exactly what would transfer.

Where do you buy?

We buy oil and gas mineral interests, royalties, and overriding royalties in producing areas across the United States. A large share of our acquisitions are in the Permian Basin of Texas and New Mexico, the Red Fork and Cherokee in Oklahoma’s Anadarko Basin, the DJ Basin in Weld County, Colorado, the Powder River Basin in Converse County, Wyoming, and the Bakken in Montana and North Dakota.

How long does closing take?

After you accept a written offer, most closings finish within a few weeks. Title work in the county usually sets the pace. We coordinate the title work and the deed. You decide whether to move ahead.
